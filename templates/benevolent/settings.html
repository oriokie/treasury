Settings are not policy. Nothing on this page can change whether a claim qualifies or what it is worth — that is what a scheme's policy decides, and it is why a policy is versioned and frozen once a case is decided under it, while these settings can be changed freely. Re-pointing an accounting mapping steers future postings only: every receipt and voucher already written carries its own fund and category, so the past is safe by construction.

Standing membership rules

These apply the policy's rules to the membership register night by night — they do not make rules of their own. Automation never overrides a person: a membership someone deliberately suspended, withdrew or expelled is left completely alone, and every rule reinstates as readily as it demotes (a member who clears their arrears goes back to active on the next run without anyone lifting a finger).

Suspension and expulsion are deliberately never automated: removing someone from a welfare scheme is a decision a person should make and be answerable for. The policy still bars their claims; automation just declines to be the one who throws them out.

When a death is recorded

A benevolent scheme exists for the death of a member or their family, so the case a death entitles the family to can be opened the moment the death is recorded — pre-filled with the event type, the beneficiary, the relationship and the policy's fixed benefit, and left as a draft for a treasurer to review and submit. It is never auto-submitted or auto-paid.
