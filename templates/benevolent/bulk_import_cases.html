Bring cases already decided before this system existed straight to their known outcome.

A case lands at its outcome, not at the start of a decision. This does not re-run a historical case through submit → assess → approve — that would judge a decades-old case against today's eligibility rules and policy, and send "your case was approved" notifications for something that happened years ago. Instead, each row records what the church's own records already show: approved amount, whether (and how much) was paid, and the final status. A paid amount is recorded as a marked historical payout, not a live payment voucher — so nothing here creates a pending expense that someone could accidentally approve and pay a second time.

Download the template, fill one row per case, and upload it here. Afterwards, use Import contribution history for the levy payments towards these cases — put the SAME reference you use in the external_reference column here into that file's case_number column, and it will find the right case without you needing to look up the newly-issued case numbers first.
