Policy profiles

Reusable constitutions — choose the nearest shape and adjust it, rather than starting from a blank page
✨ Constitution wizard ← Schemes

A profile governs nothing. Applying one creates a draft policy on a scheme, which a human still reads and publishes. That is why profiles can be edited and copied freely, while a published policy version can never be changed.

Which pattern fits your church?

Every church welfare scheme this module has been built for so far fits one of three shapes. Renewal and a registration fee are separate, independent choices layered on top of whichever shape you pick — not a fourth shape of their own — so a policy can mix and match them freely once you start from the nearest profile below.

Collects a regular (usually monthly) contribution from every member, and pays a fixed amount when someone is bereaved {% for p in profiles %}{% if p.name == "Monthly dues, fixed benefit" %}Monthly dues, fixed benefit{% endif %}{% endfor %}
Only asks members to contribute when a case actually happens — a per-case amount (e.g. KES 500 each), a fixed lump sum paid to the bereaved, and whatever the levy collects beyond that stays in the fund ("the kitty") for next time {% for p in profiles %}{% if p.name == "Per-case levy (harambee)" %}Per-case levy (harambee){% endif %}{% endfor %}
Wants both — regular dues and a top-up levy when a case happens {% for p in profiles %}{% if p.name == "Hybrid — monthly dues plus a levy" %}Hybrid — monthly dues plus a levy{% endif %}{% endfor %}

A registration fee, and whether renewal is required each year, are both switches on the policy form regardless of which of these you start from — turn either on or off to match your own constitution, they don't determine which row above fits you.

Not sure any of these fit — a scheme funded from donations alone, with no member contribution at all? Set Contribution mode to "No contributions (funded from elsewhere)" on any profile's policy form; the committee can still approve and pay a case straight from the fund's own balance whenever it chooses to, levy or no levy.
