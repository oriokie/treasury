Groups are balanced on both giving capability and size: members are seeded heaviest-first into the lightest group (with an even member count), then capability is fine-tuned by swapping members between the richest and poorest groups. Where giving is very skewed (one member giving most of it), that member's group is unavoidably heavier — but everyone else is spread evenly and sizes stay within one member. The download lists each member with their phone number (where known) and group.
