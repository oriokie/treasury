Leader paid from personal funds?

Create a payable — not a staff advance. Staff advances are church money first; this is the reverse: the church owes them.

  1. Add them once as a supplier (category Individuals, tag {{ reimburse_tag.name }}) if they are reimbursed more than once. One-offs can stay as a typed name only.
  2. Already recorded the spend as an expense? Open that expense and choose Raise payable, or use Link expense on a payable to attach the payment.
  3. Record the payable: supplier (or name), fund that benefited, amount, receipt.
  4. When you refund them, settle the payable — the expense hits the fund and the liability clears.

Empty petty tin topped up personally? The float goes negative and bank reconciliation shows refund due until you repay or bank-top-up the tin.
