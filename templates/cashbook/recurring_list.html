A schedule doesn't change balances on its own — pressing Generate due creates real expense entries on each due date (every Sabbath, or the chosen day each month), which then go through approval and post to the ledger like any expense. Locked months are skipped, and an entry is never created twice. An instalment can also be settled ahead of its due date with Pay early: the expense is dated the day the money leaves, but records which instalment it settles, so the schedule will not charge that period again.
