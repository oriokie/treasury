What belongs here?
Loan receipts, repayments, conversions and write-offs; trust fund receipts and releases to the field; and any voucher whose category is flagged as a liability (deposit refunds, advance settlements, deferred income…). New liability categories can be added under Funds & setup → expense categories — no code change needed. Every row links to its source document, where approval and the full audit history live.
Where's the accounting?
Unchanged. These rows are the same vouchers and bank credits the ledger already posts (Loans payable, Trust payable) — this register only classifies and displays them. The trial balance, fund balances, and every statement are exactly as before.
