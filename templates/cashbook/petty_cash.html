Replenish, and record what was spent

A disbursement is just an expense paid from the float. There is no separate form for it any more — record it on the ordinary expense form and tick “paid from the petty cash float”. One form, one approval trail, one place a voucher can be found — rather than two forms that wrote the same Expense row but only one of which a treasurer could attach a receipt to.

＋ Spend from the float

Topping the float up with a cheque? Write it in the payments register with source “Petty cash replenishment”. The float rises automatically when the cheque is issued — because a cheque cashed for petty cash is two movements (money leaves the bank, money arrives in the tin), and entering only one of them is how the books stop adding up.
