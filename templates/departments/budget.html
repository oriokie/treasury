Opening balance is the amount brought forward into a fund at the start of the year (last year's closing balance). It is added to receipts and reduced by expenses to give the fund's running balance. Annual budget is the total of the fund's breakdown lines — click “Edit breakdown” to set them. It's used for budget-vs-actual reports.
