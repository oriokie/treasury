{% comment %} Show pagination whenever there is more than one page. Historically this gated on `is_paginated`, which only Django's ListView sets automatically. Eleven modules build their Paginator by hand and pass `page_obj` without `is_paginated`, so the controls silently rendered nothing on 26 pages — including the benevolent member list, where a large congregation meant most members were simply unreachable.
